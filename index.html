💬 Dave Robertson's Travel Tips

From Dave & family's Europe trip — emails forwarded 30 Mar / 12 Apr 2026

London

  • Kids loved these Natural History Museum, Science Museum, Hummingbird Bakery, Harrods, riding red buses & the Tube, Sainsbury's meal deal in a park, Buckingham Palace tour, Changing of the Guard, Matilda musical, M&M factory, HMS Belfast, Churchill War Rooms, London Eye, Big Ben.
  • Churchill War Rooms combo deal There's a deal combining Churchill War Rooms + HMS Belfast + Windsor Castle — worth checking.
  • Camden Town Dave thinks the kids would've liked Camden — it's on our itinerary Day 1!
  • Sky Garden / similar Dave recommends Sky Garden or "a similar experience" for rooftop views. Free but needs booking.
  • Traditional pub lunch A classic London experience — fit one in during a free afternoon.
  • Borough Markets Dave's kids weren't fans (busy + jetlagged) — worth considering timing given we arrive Day 1.

Paris — Day 1 suggestions (from Dave)

  • Arc de Triomphe — climb to top €32, 284 steps, open 10am-11pm. 30 min walk from Eiffel Tower or quick metro. Worth doing for the views. paris-arc-de-triomphe.fr
  • Walk Champs-Elysées + Le Marché des Enfants Rouges Oldest food market in Paris, on the way to Montmartre. Good lunch option.
  • Montmartre & Sacré-Coeur Sit on the steps, check out artists' stalls. Watch for conmen/pickpockets at Sacré-Coeur.
  • Moulin Rouge (show only from 9pm) Dinner & show from 7pm. Needs advance booking. moulinrouge.fr

Paris — Day 2 suggestions (from Dave)

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral Free entry, open 8am-7pm. Reserve online. Tour ~30 mins. notredamedeparis.fr
  • Macaroon cooking class — Maison Flueret 9am, 2.5 hours. 3 Rue des 3 Portes, 75005. Dave's family booked this.
  • Eiffel Tower at 12:30pm Book level 2 via lift, then up to top. Allow 3 hours. Can walk down from 2nd level (674 steps). toureiffel.paris
  • Louvre Book timed tickets in advance (€44, kids free). Mona Lisa, Venus de Milo, Winged Victory. Free entry first Friday of the month 6-9pm. louvre.fr
  • Sunset Seine cruise at 5pm Book open ticket in advance. Eiffel Tower lights show every hour after dark. vedettesdupontneuf.com
  • Dinner in Le Marais Our Airbnb is in Le Marais — great restaurants nearby.

Paris — Optional extras

  • Galeries Lafayette Haussmann rooftop Free rooftop views across Opera Garnier, Eiffel Tower and Montmartre.
  • Jardin du Luxembourg Near Notre-Dame and the cooking class area.
  • Catacombs €29, open 9:45am-8:30pm, ~20 min from Notre-Dame by metro. Allow 45 mins. catacombes.paris.fr

Rome

  • Colosseum — book early, avoid midday Book through "Walks" or "Liv Tours" (smaller groups, great reviews). Go first thing or at sunset.
  • Vatican — book a tour Dave walked past but couldn't get in without booking. Prioritise Sistine Chapel. Consider Liv Tours.
  • Cooking class at Eat & Walk Italy Kids loved it — make pasta and tiramisu from scratch. eatandwalkitaly.it
  • Trastevere, Trevi Fountain (go early), Spanish Steps Trastevere great for wandering. Trevi gets very crowded — go at sunrise. Spanish Steps: get a gelato & people watch.
  • Best pizza: Dar Poeta darpoeta.com
  • Best value: Trattoria Luzzi Via Celimontana 1 — "went back 3 times". trattorialuzzi.it
  • Best gelato: Il Gelato di Costanza Google Maps

Bologna

  • Full-day Food & Wine Tour Parmesan, balsamic & prosciutto factories + feast with wine. Highly recommended. italiandays.it — Bologna Food and Wine Tour #1
  • Portico of St Luke (666 arches) Kids may prefer the tourist train. San Luca Express
  • Best gelato: Crememeria la Vecchia Stella Google Maps
  • Pastries: Pasticceria Regina di Quadri Google Maps
  • Bakery: Forno Brisa fornobrisa.it
  • Dinner: Taverna del Postiglione tavernadelpostiglione.it

Venice

  • Vaporetto day pass Water bus covers all main spots. Things open late — don't rush.
  • Gondola rowing lesson rowvenice.org
  • Private water taxi airport transfer Fixed price, ask hotel to book. Cool experience.

Amalfi Coast

  • Traffic is crazy in Amalfi — use boats Dave suggests taking the train to Salerno then ferrying to whichever Amalfi town you stay in. Much easier than road.

General Italy Tips

  • Prep kids for lots of walking Especially Rome. Comfortable shoes essential.
  • Follow "Untold Italy" on Facebook Great resources, tips and podcast.
  • Book accommodation via booking.com with Shopback Extra 10-15% cashback via shopback.com.au
  • Italian trains: Trainline for planning, Trenitalia to book direct thetrainline.com
  • Wanderlog app Free app for tracking & changing itinerary, getting ideas for each town. wanderlog.com
