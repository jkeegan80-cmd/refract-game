The plan for tonight

It's — in Paris. Sunset is at 9:54pm, with the sky staying bright until roughly 10:40pm. —

Aim to be on the Sacré-Cœur forecourt by about 9pm. That leaves time to settle in for the light show as the city glows and the basilica turns gold. Everything before it is sequenced so the ticketed indoor sights come while they're still open — the Panthéon (last entry ~5.45pm) and the free Petit Palais (Sat to 8pm) are your afternoon anchors.

Two things you told me, baked in: the Louvre is a garden walk-through only (no interior — tickets sold out), and the Eiffel Tower is dropped from today since you're ascending tomorrow — though you'll see it beautifully from the Left Bank and the bridges.

Getting home: you'll finish in Montmartre near 10pm. It's lovely and busy at sunset, but plan a taxi or Métro down from Abbesses or Anvers rather than walking through Pigalle late. Carry water — it's a hot one.
